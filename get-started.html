six steps / no account needed plug and play

get-started/

From download to desktop in four moves — plus two more if you want to build the ISO yourself or follow the AI layer. No accounts, no network during install, no waiting on mirrors.

Download the ISO · 1.70 GiB Back to overview

  • 01run/

    write-the-iso/

    Download the 2026.10.24 ISO and write it to a USB drive with any imaging tool. Check the SHA-256 against the release notes first — it takes a second and it is the whole trust model.

    freeGet the ISO

  • 02run/

    boot-the-live-usb/

    Plug the stick in and power on. BIOS and UEFI both boot straight into a full XFCE desktop with autologin — nothing is installed, nothing on your disk is touched. Explore first; decide later.

    plug & playRelease notes

  • 03install/

    install-offline/

    When you are ready, launch Calamares. The full-disk install copies the live system straight to disk — no mirrors, no network setup — and finished in about four minutes in our verified VMware run.

    ~4 min · verifiedHow it works

  • 04install/

    first-boot/

    Reboot into the installed system. Two known leftovers are documented, not hidden: a harmless live-user account and an uninitialized pacman keyring — one command initializes it.

    known issues, listedRead the notes

  • 05build/

    build-from-source/

    Clone the repository and assemble the ISO yourself on an Arch host: the archiso 91 profile, three owned components, and ./build.sh with its validation steps.

    openOpen the repository

  • 06build/

    watch-aira/

    The AI-on-OS layer is the destination, and it is in development. Nothing ships until it can be inspected, attributed, and turned off. The roadmap says exactly where it stands.

    plannedSee the roadmap
